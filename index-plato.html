What We Got Wrong

Lazy evaluation speedup: 55,000× Actual: 0.1-0.2×. The benchmark was timing code that never ran. We caught this ourselves and published the retraction. retracted
Bounded drift: nε holds for all walks Zero violations on closed cycles. Open walks: still under investigation. The closed-cycle result is solid. The open question is genuinely open. partial
AVX-512 speeds up everything Projection: ~2.1×. Verification: ~2.4×. Encoding: slower. Integer modular arithmetic doesn't vectorize. We published the slowdown alongside the speedups. mixed

What We Got Right

Eisenstein LUT constraint checking: 512 bytes, L1 cache resident, 500-800M ops/sec. Dodecet-Bloom speedup 2000-28000× (we originally claimed 12.8× — understated). shipped
Q(ζ₁₅) unified field: both Eisenstein (ω=ζ₁₅⁵) and Penrose (φ related to ζ₁₅) live in the 15th cyclotomic field. All 9 claims verified, error < 1e-15. proven
WASM constraint checking: 1.4KB binary. Pure integer arithmetic, no trig, no lookup tables. Runs in any browser. shipped
24-core scaling: Eisenstein snap near-linear at 18.9×. Constraint linear check 23.4× at 24 threads. benchmarked

Honest Architecture

PLATO is not a knowledge graph. It's a room-and-tile store with provenance. Append-only. Single server. No vector search yet. No conflict resolution. Unlike session-scoped systems (MemGPT, Zep), PLATO persists across context resets at fleet scale with provenance and trust tracking. We built it simple because simple survives.
